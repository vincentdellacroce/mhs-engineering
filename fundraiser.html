The Objective

Why We Need Your Support

The MHS Engineering Club is entering an elite regional engineering and design tournament at the conclusion of next school year. This event offers our students an incredible opportunity to apply classroom mechanics, programming, and hardware workflows to dynamic, real-world competitive applications.

As a completely student-driven project, funding structural raw elements, processing boards, custom component sourcing, and traveling arrangements relies entirely on our amazing local community and partners.
