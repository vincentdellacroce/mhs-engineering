2026–2027 Campaign

Why We Need Your Support

The Morris County Engineering Cup (MCEP) is a year-long competition for high school students across Morris County during the 2026–2027 school year. Students work individually or in small teams (2–3 members) to design, prototype, and build real-world engineering solutions for local community challenges.

At the end of the school year, student projects are evaluated by professional engineers. Your support ensures that components, microcontrollers, testing hardware, and event operations remain accessible to all participants free of charge.

How Campaign Funds Are Used

Every dollar raised toward our $5,000 goal directly impacts students and our local community:

  • 1. Student Scholarships
    Awarded to 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place winning teams to support their future STEM education.
  • 2. Community FoodBank of NJ Donation
    A portion of raised funds directly supports local food security initiatives in New Jersey.
  • 3. Club Operations & Event Materials
    Covers prototyping components, sensors, microcontrollers, and venue hosting costs.
  • 4. Future Competition Reserve
    Remaining surplus establishes a baseline fund to launch the next school year's competition.

Scholarships & Recognition

Rewarding student excellence with educational awards that empower young innovators to pursue college STEM pathways.

Community Giving

Extending civic impact by donating a dedicated portion of competition funding directly to the Community FoodBank of New Jersey.

Equal STEM Access

Removing financial barriers so every participating student receives the hardware, tools, and mentorship needed to succeed.
