The Objective

Why We Need Your Support

The MCEP is a comprehensive, school-year-long engineering competition bringing together Morris County high schools. The event offers individual students or small teams (2-3 members) a unique framework to scale classroom concepts up into functional, real-world competitive installations.

At the close of the academic year, every project undergoes professional, rigorous evaluation by structural, hardware, and system design engineers. To ensure equal accessibility for all participant criteria, funding raw components, computational cores, specialized assemblies, and operations rests entirely on the shoulders of our local partners and donors.

The Financial Breakdown

Our baseline funding model rewards excellence, directly feeds the regional ecosystem, and funds engineering operations. Depending on finalized team structures, the funds scale across these parameters:

  • 1st Place Scholarship Tier $1,000 per student (Total range depending on team sizes)
    $1,000 - $3,000
  • 2nd Place Scholarship Tier $750 per student (Total range depending on team sizes)
    $750 - $2,250
  • 3rd Place Scholarship Tier $500 per student (Total range depending on team sizes)
    $500 - $1,500
  • Community FoodBank of NJ Donation (CFBNJ) 20% base commitment + half of remaining undistributed pool funds
    20% Baseline+
  • Morristown Engineering Club Operations Half of remaining balance used for structural kits, hardware, and transport
    Remaining Pool Split

Community Integration

A locked 20% minimum baseline of all funds raised goes directly to support the Community FoodBank of New Jersey (CFBNJ) to target food insecurity across our regional sectors alongside our infrastructural works.

Remaining Pool Split

Once final award distributions are assessed based on individual or group counts, the remaining asset pool is split exactly 50/50 between expanding CFBNJ relief resources and funding local Morristown High School Engineering raw materials.
